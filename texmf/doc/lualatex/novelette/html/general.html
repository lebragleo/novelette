Overview

To use Novelette, you will need a only a small part of the enormous TeXlive software. You will need a program that can view PDF. If the book interior has images, you will need software for creating png images, and will probably need the ImageMagick program.

 

Platform Support

TOP

If you can install TeXlive 2025 or later, with LuaTeX support, then you can use Novelette. The installation page provides details.

If your platform is Windows, and your book has images, then you may need to use its Linux subsytem (Windows 10+) for full benefit. This is explained on the graphics page.

 

Writing/Editing Software

TOP

Novelette does not know, or care, what you use for writing. It may be a plain text editor, or a word processor. But your text must be saved (or exported) as plain text, with UTF‑8 (Unicode) encoding, without BOM (byte order mark).

  • If you already wrote your book (with a word processor), do not depair. Simply load your files into the software, and export them as plain text, utf‑8 encoding, without BOM. Any modern word processor can do that.

  • The machine that you use for writing your text, and the machine that you use for processing it with Novelette, may use different operating systems. However, best results require you to edit your text after it has been processed, so be sure that you know how to do that on any machine.

  • If your operating system is Windows, do not use Notepad. A good plain text editor is the free Notepad++ software. Be sure it is configured for utf‑8, without BOM.

  • On Linux, the Novelette developer uses the nano text editor.

  • Caution: If you use a word processor, be sure that your text is saved/exported as plain text, not as LaTeX. You do not want the software to add its own LaTeX code!

 

LaTeX GUI Programs

TOP

Most LaTeX GUI programs (such as TeXshop, TeXstudio) are designed for creating academic papers with mathematics. They offer many things that Novelette cannot use, and fail to understand Novelette custom code. Avoid them.

The very basic TeXworks GUI can be used. Or, no GUI at all.

 

Print-On-Demand (POD)

TOP

Novelette expects that your book will be printed and distributed as print-on-demand. It can directly produce PDF/X‑1a:2001 format, which is often requested by POD services.

Since POD is notorious for lighter-than-expected text, Novelette's fonts are designed to take that into account.

Choose off-white (cream, creme) paper. Not white. Unless you are a commercial professional who knows all about it, do not enroll in "extended distribution" or anything similar.

 

Fonts and Packages

TOP

Novelette is self-contained. It has been created for its intended purpose only. You cannot add other fonts or packages. LaTeX commands such as \includepackage are diabled.

If Novelette cannot do what you want, then you don't need it. Seriously! The developer has spent many hours looking through the murder mystery, science fiction, and general fiction shelves at the public library. Novelette can do all but the most exotic of them. It can also do some of the popular nonfiction.

Some locations, such as the title page and chapter openers, can benefit from dramatic use of text. Novelette provides a few useful fonts for that purpose. Otherwise, use images. Since images are raster, you may use any fonts that your graphics program can read (and that you are licensed to use for that purpose), and you can add non-font decorations.

 

Graphics

TOP

Graphics must be non-color png raster format, without transparency. Details are provided on the graphics page. In particular, no TiKz drawings, and no EPS or PDF inclusion.

If your book is very image-intensive (such as graphic novel), then do not use Novelette. For that, you need a page layout program. If your book contains photographs that must look like photographs (instead of vaguely like them), then you cannot use Novelette, because that requires a commercial print technology that Novelette does not support.

Novelette cannot do color cover artwork. That's a job for your graphics program.

 

Language Support

TOP

Novelette is optimized for English. It also supports most (not all) major Western European languages, but no Eastern European or other world languages. If your language is supported, but is not English, be sure to review Novelette's capabilities before you write your book.

If your book is written in English, you must choose whether its hyphenation uses American or British hyphenation patterns. Most other languages have no regional variants.

If Novelette fonts have the characters you need, and the hyphenation pattern for your language, that is all you can expect. Many languages have specific typesetting rules. Although Novelette knows a few of them, you may need to manually solve language-specific typesetting problems yourself. The Novelette developer will not solve them for you.

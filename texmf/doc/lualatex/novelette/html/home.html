Overview

Novelette is a custom LuaLaTeX document class, designed for one purpose only: original fiction, printed to paper, no color. It assumes that your book will be softcover, manufactured using print-on-demand; but it may be useful for other purposes.

If your work is an Ebook, nonfiction, academic, or a reprint of someone else's work, then do not use Novelette; it will not have features that you need. In particular, Novelette does not support math.

If your book is nonfiction, but looks and reads like fiction, then perhaps you can use Novelette. That might includes memoirs or other history.

Do you already have good knowledge of LaTeX? If so, then forget most of what you know. And, do not look at online LaTeX how-to pages; they do not correspond to Novelette.
